How to read this chart

This chart shows, for each month from August 2024 to April 2026, how many PSA plants submitted an ONA mock-drill and whether they were Functional or Not-Functional at the time of that submission.

  • Green bar segments = plants that reported Functional that month. Taller green = more plants working well.
  • Red bar segments = plants that reported Not-Functional. Taller red = more plants broken.
  • Dots above bars = average oxygen purity (%) for functional plants that month. Higher dots = better purity. The WHO safe threshold is 93% — a dashed line marks this.

How to drill down: Click any monthly bar to see individual plant-level results for that month. Each dot represents one plant. Hover over a dot to see facility name, purity, and status. Click ← All months to return to the overview.

What to look for: If red bars are growing over time, more plants are breaking down. A sudden drop in purity dots means quality problems across your district. Use the District filter above to focus on one area.

How to read this chart

This shows the average oxygen purity (%) of functional plants over time, based on ONA mock-drill readings. Each plant contributes up to 3 readings per drill; the average of those is shown.

  • The line = monthly average purity across all functional plants in your filter.
  • Red shaded band below 90% = danger zone — purity too low for safe medical use.
  • Dashed line at 93% = WHO minimum standard for medical-grade oxygen. Plants below this need attention.
  • Dots on the line = click to drill into individual plant readings for that month.

How to drill down: Click any dot on the line to see a scatter view of individual plants for that month — each dot is one plant. Hover for facility name and purity value. Click ← Overview to return.

What to act on: If the line dips below 93%, identify which plants are pulling the average down using the drill-down. Plants recording 0% purity are excluded — these appear as "Purity = 0" in the KPI tiles above.

How to use this visualisation

This panel surfaces ONA mock-drill dimensions not shown in the charts above — funding scheme performance, the reasons behind non-functional status, and how recently each plant submitted a drill.

  • By Funding Scheme: Each card shows functional %, average purity, and complaint count for plants under that scheme. The coloured bar is the functional rate. Click a card to see all plants in that scheme.

How to read this funnel

This funnel shows how many plants make it through each stage of the e-Upkaran QR Code system — from being listed in the registry to actively submitting drill data. Each row is a stage, and the bar width shows what proportion of total plants reached that stage.

  • Total matched plants = all 471 plants in the registry (the starting point).
  • Has QR Code = plants assigned a scannable QR Code in the Stock on Hand register. Plants without QR codes cannot submit e-Upkaran data.
  • Verified inventory = plants whose physical existence has been confirmed by a MOIC officer. Only verified plants can officially submit drill reports.
  • Verified + reporting (last 90 days) = verified plants that have actually submitted at least one e-Upkaran drill in the past 90 days. This is the headline coverage figure.
  • Verified, not reporting = the coverage gap — plants that qualify but aren't submitting data. These are the priority action list below.

Status vs Trend toggle: The Status button shows the current snapshot. The Trend button shows how the reporting rate has changed month by month — useful for spotting whether coverage is improving or declining.

Drill down: Click any funnel row to see the individual plants at that stage. Click ← Overview to go back.

Target: The percentage of verified plants that are reporting should be as high as possible. A low reporting rate means data quality problems — you cannot track plant health for non-reporting facilities. Start with plants in the "Verified, not reporting" row.

User Guide — how this dashboard is organised
This dashboard tracks Rajasthan's PSA oxygen plants across four independent data systems — ONA mock-drills, e-Upkaran (EU) mock-drills, the asset registry / QR programme, and the complaint management system. Each tab is deliberately built around a single clear question, so figures from one source are never silently mixed with another. Use this page to understand what each tab answers and which data it draws on. For the exact formula behind any individual KPI, open the Methodology tab.
1 · ONA Mockdrill
Everything reported through ONA mock-drills — shown for all plants that submitted a drill, not limited to those matched across systems.
  • KPIs: total ONA plants, Functional / Non-Functional counts, average purity of functional plants.
  • Functionality over time: monthly stacked bars of Functional vs Non-Functional drill submissions.
  • Purity distribution: monthly average oxygen purity against the 93% WHO threshold.
  • ONA Plant Intelligence: scheme performance, Non-Functional reason breakdown, and drill recency.
Source: ONA mock-drill data only. No complaint or registry data is mixed in.
2 · EU Mockdrill
Everything reported through e-Upkaran mock-drills — shown for all plants present in EU drill data.
  • KPIs: total EU plants, Functional / Functional Installed / In-Complaint counts, purity ≥ 93%, and not-running (purity = 0).
  • Equipment status over time: monthly equipment-status breakdown.
  • Purity trend: monthly average purity from EU readings.
  • EU Mock-Drill Intelligence: safety observations, running-hours distribution, and drill recency.
Source: e-Upkaran mock-drill data only. No complaint or registry data is mixed in.
3 · Complaints Status
The only tab that crosses two sources — it links plant functionality with the complaint record. Two inner sub-tabs keep the two cross-references clearly separate:
  • ONA Mock-Drill × Complaints: for plants with a verified-clean ONA↔registry mapping, crosses the latest ONA status against complaint history (open complaints, resolution time, and gaps such as "broken but no complaint raised").
  • e-Upkaran × Complaints: for plants present in EU drill data, crosses the latest EU equipment status against the complaint record.
Source: ONA or EU mock-drills joined with complaint records. Ambiguous multi-plant locations are excluded from the ONA cross.
4 · QR Code Coverage
How far each plant has progressed through the e-Upkaran QR / verification programme, from registry entry to active reporting.
  • KPIs: total in registry, has QR / no QR, verified inventory, and the verified-and-reporting rate.
  • Coverage funnel: registry → has QR → verified → ever reported → reporting recently, with a month-by-month trend view.
  • Priority action list: verified plants that have stopped reporting, sorted by longest silence.
Source: asset registry + reporting status. No complaint data is mixed in.
5 · Data Report
A transparency view of how the datasets were matched together — mapping coverage, matched / unmatched / ambiguous plants, and an Excel export of the underlying tables.
6 · Methodology
The reference guide for every KPI and calculation shown across the dashboard — definitions, formulas, thresholds, and how each indicator is derived.

Indicator methodology
Reference guide for every KPI shown across the three dashboard pages. Click the → button on any indicator to jump directly to its section here. Data as of 17 Apr 2026 · 471 matched plants · Rajasthan.
Data sources & plant matching

The dashboard links four independent data systems on a common plant identifier. A plant is considered matched only when it appears in all relevant registries without ambiguity.

SystemWhat it capturesKey field used
ONAMonthly mock-drill submissions by plant operators — functionality status + purity readingsPlant code → linked to registry
e-UpkaranAsset-level equipment status, QR Code scanning, purity at time of drillQR / QR Code → Stock on Hand
Complaint managementComplaint lifecycle: raised → attended → repaired → MOIC verifiedPlant ID → timestamps per stage
Asset registryMaster list of PSA plants with capacity (LPM), district, scheme, facility nameCanonical plant ID
Plants that exist in only one or two systems are excluded from cross-dataset indicators to avoid false positives. The "471 matched plants" figure refers to plants present and linkable across all active datasets.
ONA functional / non-functional status Page 1

ONA collects one mock-drill submission per plant per reporting cycle. Each submission records a binary status: Functional or Not-Functional, along with up to three purity readings.

Latest status = status field of the drill record with the most recent submission timestamp for that plant
IndicatorDefinition
Total ONA plantsCount of matched plants with ≥ 1 ONA drill record. No date filter applied.
Functional (ONA)Plants where latest drill status = Functional. One value per plant — the most recent submission wins.
Non-functional (ONA)Plants where latest drill status = Not-Functional. Same logic as above.
Date filters on Page 1 do not affect these status counts — they always reflect the latest drill regardless of when it was submitted. Date filters only affect complaint-derived indicators.
e-Upkaran equipment status Page 2

e-Upkaran records one status per plant per drill event. The dashboard uses the latest drill record within any active date filter. Four statuses are possible:

StatusMeaningDashboard grouping
FunctionalPlant operational and delivering oxygen at standard purityFunctional (green)
Functional InstalledPhysically installed, power on, but full commissioning pendingFunctional (blue on P2, grouped as functional in bar chart)
In ComplaintA complaint is active against this plant at time of drillNon-functional (red)
Non Functional RepairablePlant is down but assessed as repairableNon-functional (amber)
Functional count = plants where latest EU drill status ∈ {Functional, Functional Installed}, filtered by district + EU drill date range + EU status filter
The "Equipment status over time" chart collapses Functional + Functional Installed into one green segment and In Complaint + NF Repairable into one red segment for visual clarity.
Oxygen purity Pages 1 & 2

Purity is measured as the percentage of oxygen in the output gas. The WHO threshold for medical-grade oxygen is ≥ 93%. A secondary alert threshold of 90% is also shown.

IndicatorCalculation
Avg purity — functional plants (ONA)Mean of per-plant purity values, where each plant's value = average of up to 3 readings in its latest drill. Plants reporting purity = 0 are excluded. Only Functional-status plants included.
Purity ≥ 93% (EU)Count of plants where the latest e-Upkaran drill purity reading ≥ 93%. Plants with purity = 0 are excluded from this count.
Purity = 0 (not running)Plants whose latest EU drill recorded purity = 0, indicating the plant was not operational at the time of measurement. These are excluded from all purity averages.
Plant purity (ONA) = mean(reading₁, reading₂, reading₃) from latest drill, where readings > 0 and ≤ 100%
Overall avg purity = Σ(plant_purity × 1) ÷ count(plants with valid purity)
Outlier readings above 100% (sensor errors) are clamped to 100% before averaging. Readings of exactly 0 are treated as "not operational" and excluded.
Complaint counts Pages 1 & 2

Complaints are raised by plant operators or facility staff when a plant is non-functional or has a known fault. The complaint date filter on each page restricts which complaints are counted.

IndicatorDefinition
Plants with ≥1 complaintCount of plants that have at least one complaint with a raise date within the selected date range.
Active complaintPlants where at least one complaint within the date range has status ≠ Resolved at the time of the data extract.
has_active_complaint = any(complaint.status ≠ "Resolved") for complaints with raise_date within filter range
A complaint is considered "active" based on its status at the time of data extraction (17 Apr 2026), not at the time it was raised. Applying a narrow date range therefore shows complaints raised in that window that are still unresolved today.
Complaint resolution time Pages 1 & 2

Resolution time measures how long it takes to fully close a complaint from the moment it is raised to MOIC sign-off. Only resolved complaints with a valid MOIC close date are included.

Total resolution time (days) = MOIC_close_date − complaint_raise_date
StageMeasured as
Response timeDate technician first attended − raise date
Repair timeRepair close date − technician attend date
Verification timeMOIC sign-off date − repair close date
Total resolutionMOIC sign-off date − raise date (sum of all stages)
Avg resolution time = mean(total_resolution_days) across resolved complaints within filter range
Complaints missing any stage timestamp are still included in the total resolution average if they have a valid MOIC close date. Stage-level averages exclude complaints where that specific stage timestamp is missing.
Discrepancy flags Pages 1 & 2

Discrepancies highlight plants where the self-reported functional status in ONA or e-Upkaran conflicts with the complaint record. These are key flags for field follow-up.

FlagConditionInterpretation
Functional + open complaintLatest ONA/EU status = Functional AND ≥1 active complaint in date rangePlant self-reported as working but has an unresolved complaint — field data may be inaccurate or complaint not yet actioned
Non-functional, no complaintLatest ONA status = Not-Functional AND 0 complaints in date rangePlant self-reported as broken but no complaint has been raised — breakdown not escalated through formal channel
ONA vs EU status mismatch (Page 2 table)ONA status ≠ EU status for the same plant, excluding Functional InstalledTwo independent data collection systems disagree on whether the plant is working
Discrepancy counts are sensitive to the complaint date filter. Widening the date range will capture more historical complaints, potentially reducing the "no complaint" count.
QR code & inventory verification Page 3

For a plant to participate in e-Upkaran mock-drill reporting, it must have a valid QR code in the Stock on Hand register and must be MOIC-verified.

IndicatorSource fieldDefinition
Has QR codeStock on Hand → QR code fieldPlant has a non-null QR Code assigned in the asset register
No QR codeStock on Hand → QR code fieldQR field is null or blank — plant cannot be scanned in e-Upkaran
Verified inventoryStock on Hand → Inventory StatusStatus = Verified Inventory — MOIC has physically confirmed the asset exists and matches the register entry
UnverifiedStock on Hand → Inventory StatusStatus = New Inventory or Added By Service Provider — not yet confirmed by MOIC
Verified % of QR plants = Verified inventory count ÷ Has QR count × 100
Reporting coverage Page 3

Reporting coverage measures what share of MOIC-verified plants are actively submitting mock-drill data to e-Upkaran. This is the headline coverage metric for the QR Code programme.

% verified plants reporting = Verified + reporting ÷ Total verified × 100
IndicatorDefinition
Verified + reportingPlants with Inventory Status = Verified AND at least one mock-drill record in e-Upkaran within the selected drill date range (default: last 90 days from 17 Jan 2026)
Verified, not reportingPlants with Inventory Status = Verified but NO mock-drill record within the date range — the coverage gap
% verified plants reportingHeadline coverage rate. Colour-coded: ≥60% green, 40–59% amber, <40% red
The denominator is always verified plants, not all matched plants. Unverified plants are excluded because they cannot legitimately submit drills. The default 90-day window uses the data extract date (17 Apr 2026) as the reference point.
